# Assessment of the native allocation policy

The four independent reviews support a narrower and more precise account of the policy. The native allocator is a qualified, generation-scoped, bounded integer search with conservative serial calendar placement. It protects a feasible incumbent and reports a search limit without a numerical optimality gap. An empty admission is feasible when no useful qualified work fits. The proxy objective and native objective are different models, so neither supplies a quality certificate for the other.

The accepted corrections are part of the current decision rule. Native dominant shares use approved episode endowments rather than residual capacity. Same-episode settled use and unresolved started holds contribute once to project history. Project priority, entitlement weight, minimum workflow, and role floors come from the profile; task declarations may strengthen assurance but cannot weaken profile policy. Absolute parsed deadlines drive ordering and feasibility, while deadline-risk and consequence do not receive a second reward inside useful value. Repair checks follow the creator of the artifact they inspect, including blocked creators and debugger-produced repairs. Holds debit only their matching reset instance, and pacing retains signed deficits instead of hiding overcommitment at zero.

The policy deliberately remains priority-first. Dominant-share fairness is a later comparison among equally useful qualified allocations, and replacement is scoped to the supplied generation and workspace. Reservations outside that scope remain fixed rather than being silently released. The system therefore makes no claim of global portfolio fairness, dominant-resource-fair sharing incentive, strategy-proofness, envy-freeness, or starvation freedom. With indivisible bundles, several attractive fairness properties cannot generally be obtained together; Parkes, Procaccia, and Shah give the relevant mechanism-design boundaries for discrete allocation ([ACM TEAC paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/2739040)). Those results guide future policy choices but do not certify this allocator.

Deadline-first placement addresses the concrete priority-inversion counterexample raised by the reviews. It is a conservative heuristic for nonpreemptive visits, gapped active calendars, DAG dependencies, account lanes, and reset bounds. Classical earliest-deadline results do not automatically extend to that setting. The emitted schedule is feasible under the declared serial policy; no parallel job-shop or makespan optimum is claimed.

Expected, reserve, lower-envelope, and upper-envelope native quantities remain separate per native unit and episode. Adding deterministic simultaneous lower or upper bounds is arithmetically valid regardless of dependence, but the resulting totals are not confidence intervals unless their inputs and simultaneous coverage justify that reading. The current search does not implement a fitted uncertainty budget or probability guarantee. Bertsimas and Sim develop budgeted robustness for linear and mixed-integer settings under explicit uncertainty-set assumptions ([Operations Research article](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/opre.1030.0065), [author-hosted paper](https://www.mit.edu/~dbertsim/papers/melvyn/The-Price-Of-Robustness-OR52.pdf)); their framework is a research direction here rather than a description of current reserves.

Exact route qualification remains an eligibility gate. Benchmark transfer, calibrated role evidence, and distinct creator/checker bundles do not establish a probability of team success or independence of errors. Provisional evidence permits only bounded, observable, low-consequence real work. Known access, capability, quota, calendar, and project-policy facts are read directly. Missing facts defer affected work; no synthetic calibration jobs or unnecessary estimators are introduced.

Several mathematical directions could improve later policies if their assumptions match observed operation. Karma carries allocation memory through credits to address dynamic sharing across time ([OSDI 2023](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi23/presentation/vuppalapati)). Bandits with replenishing knapsacks studies stochastic and adversarial cases under explicit replenishment assumptions that would need to match native reset behavior ([primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.08470)). Nonstationary resource allocation needs explicit variation assumptions and feedback, as in Lyu and Cheung ([ICML 2023](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/lyu23a.html)). Adaptive evaluation and optional stopping require confidence procedures designed for repeated looks; relevant foundations include anytime-valid inference ([preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.09540)) and time-uniform confidence sequences ([preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.08240)). Network calculus can separate burst and rate constraints from cumulative quota when providers expose those facts ([Jean-Yves Le Boudec’s primary materials](https://leboudec.github.io/netcal/)). None of these sources justifies adding an arbitrary uncertainty budget, aging constant, exploration rule, or estimator without matching data and policy assumptions.

The retained reports are independent critiques, not votes. Their useful counterexamples shaped the corrections above. Claims that standard EDF proves optimality here, that deterministic interval sums require comonotonicity, or that Bertsimas-Sim is limited to continuous linear programs are rejected. Broader fairness, stochastic robustness, online learning, and parallel scheduling remain explicit research possibilities rather than present guarantees.
